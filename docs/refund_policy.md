# Refund Policy

- Refunds are available within 30 days of the delivery date.
- The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
- Once a refund is approved, the amount is credited back to the original
  payment method within 5-7 business days.
- Digital products and gift cards are not eligible for refunds.
- To start a refund, provide your order id and the reason for the return.
